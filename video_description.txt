=== Corrections ===

Corrections:

0:14:53 - While Steve Jobs was assigned the task, I should have noted that Jobs' friend and assistant Steve Wozniak actually did the work to make Breakout, and deserves the credit for the game.

0:24:18 - Fantasy Zone was released in 1986, not in 1979 as shown on screen.

0:33:52 - Metroid was released in 1986, not 1987 as shown on screen. 1987 is its US NES release year, while 1986 was its original Famicom Disk System release year in Japan

1:39:45 - I refer to the academic \"Dan Pincheck\". His name is actually \"Dan Pinchbeck\". The subtitles have been changed to reflect this name.
